1. The short version

Julius is designed to work entirely offline. It does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not send your content to the developer or anyone else. Everything you create in the app is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The developer has no access to it and keeps no copy of it.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how the Julius Android application ("Julius", "the app", package ru.cactusteam.julius) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play and to this page.

It does not cover services provided by others, including Google Play, your device manufacturer, your mobile operator, or any apps and websites you open from Julius. Those services have their own privacy policies.

3. Who we are

Julius is developed and published by Vasiliy Potapenko, an individual developer ("we", "us", "the developer"). For questions about this policy or about privacy in Julius, write to vasiliy.potapenko@gmail.com.

4. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, request or receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • account credentials — the app has no sign-in;
  • your contacts, calendar or messages;
  • your precise or approximate location;
  • photos, camera, microphone or other content of your device;
  • advertising ID, device identifiers or other persistent identifiers;
  • your IP address — the app has no internet access and makes no network requests;
  • usage analytics, behavioural data or advertising profiles;
  • crash reports or diagnostics collected by the app;
  • payment information — the app has no in-app purchases.

Julius uses no cookies, pixels, fingerprinting or other tracking technology, neither in the app nor on this page. This page is a static document: it loads no scripts, fonts or images from other servers.

5. Data stored on your device

To do its job, Julius saves the content you create and your preferences locally:

  • boards, their names and columns;
  • tasks, including titles, notes, tags, due dates, recurrence rules and completion state;
  • completion history and boards kept in the trash for possible restore;
  • app settings, such as whether onboarding has been completed.

This data is kept in an on-device database (Room) and local preferences (DataStore), in a private directory that only Julius can access under Android's app sandbox. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties, and it remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

6. Android backup and device transfer

Julius allows Android's built-in backup features to include the app's database and preferences. If backup is enabled on your device, Android may copy that data to your personal Google Drive account, or transfer it to a new device during setup.

  • The developer cannot access these copies and is not notified when they are made.
  • The copies belong to your Google account and are handled by Google under Google's privacy policy and your device settings.
  • You can turn backup off in Android Settings (typically under System → Backup), and you can manage or delete backups in Google Drive.

7. Export and import

Julius lets you export a board to a JSON file and import it back, for example to move your data to another device.

  • Export and import use Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework). The app can only access the specific file or location you choose, and request no storage permissions.
  • The exported file is created at the destination you pick, such as Downloads or a cloud drive app. From that moment it is controlled by you and by the provider of that location.
  • Julius does not encrypt the exported file. Treat it like any other personal document and share it only with people you trust.
  • We do not receive, see or store a copy of any exported or imported file.

8. Third-party services

Julius includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-media or attribution SDKs. The app is built with general-purpose open-source libraries (such as AndroidX, Kotlin and Hilt) that run locally on your device and do not collect or transmit data for the developer.

Google Play, operated by Google LLC, distributes the app. Google may collect information about downloads and installs and, if you opt in on your device, usage and diagnostics. That processing is governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

9. Permissions

Julius requests no internet permission and no permissions that would grant access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files you have not explicitly chosen. When you export or import a board, Android's file picker gives the app temporary access to the single document you select, and only for that operation.

10. Google Play Data safety

For the purposes of Google Play's Data safety section, Julius does not collect or share any user data. No data is transmitted off your device by the app, and no data types are declared as collected.

11. Legal bases and your rights

We do not process your personal data on servers or anywhere else: all operations happen locally on your device. As a result, there is no processing by the developer that would require a legal basis in the sense of the GDPR or the UK GDPR.

The only exception is correspondence you choose to send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use them solely to reply to you and to handle any follow-up, we do not add you to mailing lists, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, to object to its processing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Because we hold no personal data about you apart from any correspondence you send us, such requests generally concern data stored locally on your device, which you control directly: you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app at any time. You may also contact us using the details below; we will respond within the period required by applicable law, and usually within one month.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not process personal data for targeted advertising.

12. Data retention and deletion

  • Content in the app is kept on your device until you delete it. Boards can be moved to the trash, restored, or deleted permanently. You can also clear all app data in Android Settings → Apps → Julius → Storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Correspondence you send us is kept only as long as needed to answer your request and for any follow-up, then deleted.
  • Backups are retained according to your Google account settings; manage or delete them in Google Drive or in your device's backup settings.

Because the developer holds no copy of your app data, there is no server-side account or database to delete. Uninstalling Julius removes its local data from your device, subject to any backups you have enabled.

13. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox, which prevents other apps from reading Julius's private storage, and by your device's screen lock and encryption. Because the app does not transmit data, there is no data in transit to intercept and no developer server that could be breached.

The security of exported files and backups depends on where you keep them. Protect your device and accounts with a strong lock screen, and store exported JSON files somewhere you trust.

14. Children's privacy

Julius is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 where local law requires a higher age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, because we do not collect personal data from anyone. If a child uses the app, the content they create stays on the device and under the control of the device owner. Parents and guardians can delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Julius.

15. International transfers

We do not transfer your data internationally, because we do not receive it. If you use Android backup, Google may store backup copies in data centers in various countries under its own terms.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will highlight it on this page and, where appropriate, in the app or the Google Play listing.
